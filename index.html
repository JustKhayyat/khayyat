Khayyat is a Palestinian producer and sound engineer, and co-founder of SVNBIRDS, known for his work with artists in the West Asia and North Africa (WANA) region and beyond. He has collaborated with leading names including Soulja, Dafencii, and Montiyago, earning a reputation as “your favorite artist’s favorite artist.”

With placements on the Hulu series Ramy and the Netflix series Clickbait, Khayyat continues to shape the sound of contemporary hip hop and trap from the region to the world.
